*A visually rich dashboard that analyzes pizza orders, delivery performance, customer locations, and monthly trends to support quick and informed business decisions.*

---

## 🧭 Project Overview

The **Pizza Sales Dashboard** provides a **clear and interactive visual summary** of pizza ordering behavior, delivery efficiency, and customer demand across locations and time periods.

This dashboard helps:
- 📌 Restaurant managers  
- 📌 Business analysts  
- 📌 Operations teams  

to **identify trends**, **optimize delivery**, and **improve customer satisfaction**.

---

## ✨ Dashboard Properties & Features:

🍕 **Orders by Pizza Size** – Understand customer size preferences  
🥦 **Orders by Pizza Type** – Compare Veg, Non-Veg, Vegan & Specialty pizzas  
🚚 **Delivery Duration by Restaurant** – Analyze average delivery time  
📅 **Monthly Order Trend** – Track order volume across months  
📍 **Top Locations by Orders** – Identify high-demand cities  
📏 **Distance vs Delivery Time** – Measure delivery delays  
🎯 **Key Insights Displayed** – Highlighted conclusions for faster decisions

## 🧩 Key Insights Summary:

✅ Medium-sized pizzas dominate customer preference  
✅ Non-Veg pizzas are the top-selling category  
✅ Delivery delays increase with distance  
✅ August is the peak demand month  
✅ Atlanta, GA is the highest-order location  
✅ Restaurant delivery efficiency varies significantly
